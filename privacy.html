Any Gym Coach: privacy policy

Effective date: PLACEHOLDER, date to follow.

What this app is

Any Gym Coach is a training app for one gym-goer at a time. It plans workouts from the equipment a gym has, and it records what you do as you train.

The short version

This app does not collect your data. It does not send your training records anywhere. There is no account to create and no sign-in. Every workout, every set, and every gym you set up stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores your gyms, your training limits, your sessions, and every set you log. All of this sits in a database on your own phone, using Apple's local storage on the device (a package called expo-sqlite). Nothing in this record is sent to a server, because the app has no server. There is no cloud copy, no backup the app makes for you, and no way for anyone else, including the people who built this app, to see it.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you use voice logging, the app asks for the microphone only when you tap to speak, and only for that moment. Speech recognition runs on the device itself. The app sets this explicitly in its own code, at the one place that starts a recognition session, so that no audio is sent over the network for transcription. What the app heard is shown back to you on screen before anything is saved; it saves nothing you have not accepted.

Accounts

There is no account. There is no sign-in. Nobody's identity is checked, because nobody's identity is collected.

Advertising, analytics, and other tracking

None. The app carries no advertising code, no analytics code, and no crash-reporting service. It does not track you, and it has nothing to share with anyone else's service, because it uses none.

Exporting your data

You can export your full training record from inside the app, as a file you can open in a spreadsheet. That export only goes where you send it: the app hands it to your phone's own share sheet, and you choose the destination, whether that is a message, an email, a file, or another app. The app itself does not send the export anywhere on your behalf.

Deleting your data

You can correct or delete anything you have recorded. Deleting a session inside the app is final: there is no bin, no undo, and no way to recover it from within the app once you confirm the deletion. If you want a copy first, export your data before you delete it. Deleting the app itself removes every record the app was holding on your phone.

The one thing that does contact a server

This app checks, automatically, each time it opens, whether a newer version of its own code is available. That check is a standard part of the platform the app is built on (a service called EAS Update, from Expo). It contacts Expo's own update server once per launch and asks a simple question: is there a newer version of the app's code for this platform, this build, and this update channel. That question carries no personal information and no training data; it identifies only the app itself, not you. Nothing about your workouts, your sets, your gyms, or your voice is part of that exchange.

Apple Health

This app does not read or write Apple Health data today. A later release plans to bring InBody and Apple Health data into the app. If that happens, this page will be updated first, and it will say plainly what is read, what is written, and why, before that release reaches your phone.

Children

This app is not directed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this page

If what the app does changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top will show when. Continuing to use the app after a change means you have seen the new page.
