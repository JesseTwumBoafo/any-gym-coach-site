Any Gym Coach: support

Any Gym Coach builds your workout from the equipment your gym has, and logs each set as you train. This page answers the questions people ask most before anyone gets in touch.

Questions people ask

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-in and no account. You can use the whole app from the first time you open it.

Where are my records kept?
On your own phone. Every gym, every session, and every set you log stays in a database on the device. Nothing is sent to a server, because the app does not have one.

How do I export my data?
Inside the app, use the export option to produce a file of your full training record. Your phone's own share sheet then lets you send that file wherever you choose, for example to an email or to a file on your phone.

How do I delete everything?
You can delete a single session inside the app, and that deletion is final: there is no undo. To remove everything at once, delete the app itself from your phone; that removes every record it was holding.
